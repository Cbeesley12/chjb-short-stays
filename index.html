Booking questions

Frequently asked questions about Doncaster serviced accommodation

These answers cover the questions contractors, families, NHS staff and longer-stay guests usually ask before booking.

How much does contractor accommodation in Doncaster cost?

Rates depend on the property, dates, team size and length of stay. CHJB Short Stays offers flexible nightly, weekly and monthly pricing, and longer bookings usually give better value. Use the booking page to check live prices or contact us for a team quote.

Is there a minimum stay?

Minimum stays can change by property and season. Short stays are available when the calendar allows, and weekly or monthly stays can be arranged for contractors, NHS staff, relocations and families.

Is serviced accommodation cheaper than a hotel in Doncaster?

For teams or longer visits, serviced accommodation is often better value than booking several hotel rooms because you get shared living space, a fully equipped kitchen, Wi-Fi, parking and bills included in one place.

Do your Doncaster properties have parking?

Yes. The properties are set up for guests who need practical parking, including contractors with vans and families travelling by car. Each listing explains the parking available before you book.

Can self-employed contractors claim accommodation as a business expense?

Accommodation for work away from home may be tax-deductible for self-employed contractors, depending on your circumstances. Please check with your accountant or HMRC before claiming costs.

How do I book accommodation for a project team?

Choose the property and dates on the booking page, or contact CHJB Short Stays with your team size, vehicles, preferred area and length of stay. We can point you to the most suitable Doncaster property.

Do you allow pets?

Pet rules can vary by property and booking length. Please ask before booking so we can confirm whether a suitable option is available.

Can I book a long stay in Doncaster?

Yes. Weekly and monthly stays are welcome when availability allows, including contractor projects, insurance stays, relocations and people between homes.
