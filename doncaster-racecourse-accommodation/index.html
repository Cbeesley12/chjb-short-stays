Race day planning

A whole house keeps the weekend easier to manage

For a race day or St Leger week booking, the accommodation can shape the whole trip. Separate hotel rooms might work for couples, but groups often need somewhere to meet, make plans, get ready, store bags and spend time together away from the course. A serviced house gives the booking one address and one shared space.

St Leger week is the time to look early. Demand around Doncaster Racecourse can rise for major race periods, and group houses are less flexible than single hotel rooms once the best dates have gone.
