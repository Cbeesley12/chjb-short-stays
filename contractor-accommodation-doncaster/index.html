Why serviced accommodation works

More useful than separate hotel rooms

For a working team, hotel rooms can become awkward quickly. People need somewhere to cook, space to sit together, a place to plan the next day and parking that does not become a daily problem. A serviced house gives the team a base, not just a bed.

£

Lower cost per head

When several people share one house, the cost can be easier to manage than booking multiple hotel rooms. The guide prices are from £72, £100 and £182 per night, depending on the property and dates.

K

Cook instead of eating out

A fully equipped kitchen helps teams keep normal routines and avoid restaurant or takeaway costs every night. That matters more on long projects than it does on a weekend trip.

P

Parking for work vehicles

Free parking is a practical feature, not a luxury, when guests are travelling with vans, tools and more than one vehicle. The larger Stainforth house has a large free parking area.

W

Wi-Fi and living space

Teams can work online, speak with family, watch something together or simply get out of their bedrooms. Shared living space is one of the biggest differences from a hotel stay.

Team bookings

Send the details that actually matter

If you are booking for a crew, the fastest way to get the right option is to send the headcount, number of vehicles, arrival and departure dates, preferred property, rota pattern and whether the team needs Monday to Friday accommodation or a full seven-night stay. That gives CHJB Short Stays enough information to point you towards the right Doncaster base.

Weekly and monthly bookings can work better for longer projects because the team is not moving every few nights. For self-employed contractors, accommodation for work away from home may be an allowable business expense, depending on your circumstances. Always check with your accountant or HMRC before claiming costs.

Contractor booking FAQs

Questions for Doncaster contractor stays

Can I book accommodation for a Doncaster contractor team?

Yes. CHJB Short Stays has Doncaster-area houses suitable for teams from two guests up to larger groups, depending on the property and availability.

What information should I send for a team booking?

Send the headcount, dates, number of vehicles, preferred rota pattern, property preference and whether you need weekly or monthly pricing.

Can vans park at the Doncaster contractor houses?

The properties offer free parking, and the Stainforth houses are especially practical for working guests travelling with vans or multiple vehicles.

Can I book Monday to Friday contractor accommodation?

Ask before booking. Some dates work better for Monday to Friday rota patterns, while other projects need seven-night bookings.

Do longer contractor stays get better rates?

Longer weekly and monthly stays can work out better than short nightly stays. Check live availability or contact CHJB Short Stays with your project dates.

Can I get a receipt for work accommodation?

Bookings are handled through the direct booking system. If you need booking records for expenses, ask before confirming so the details can be handled correctly.
